Privacy

Last updated 22 September 2026

Sprout has no account, no server of its own, no analytics and no advertising. There is nothing to sign up for and no profile of you anywhere. This page says exactly what that means, including the one thing that does leave your phone.

What stays on your device

All of it, apart from the exception in the next section. Your plants and the species you chose for them, their nicknames, pots, rooms and positions on your floor plan; every photo you take; every diary entry, measurement and milestone; your name, your pets, how you described your experience; your watering history and your reminder schedule.

These live in your browser's local storage, or in the app's own storage on your phone. They are never uploaded, never backed up to us, and never seen by anyone but you. We could not retrieve them if you asked.

The one thing that leaves

If you set a location, Sprout needs a weather forecast for it, and forecasts come from Open-Meteo.

  • The coordinates are rounded first, to two decimal places — roughly a kilometre. Sprout never holds or sends a precise position, only a neighbourhood.
  • Those rounded coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo twice: once to find the name of the nearest town, and again for each forecast.
  • Nothing accompanies them. No identifier, no account, no device details beyond what any web request carries.
  • Sprout stores the result on your phone. It is not sent anywhere else, and there is nowhere else for it to go.

Setting a location is optional. The app works without one; you lose the forecast suggestions and nothing else.

Reminders

Morning reminders are scheduled by your phone's own operating system, not by a server. There is no push service and no subscription, so no third party learns when your plants need watering — or that you use Sprout at all. Sprout works out what is due on the device and hands the list to the OS to deliver.

Permissions, and why

  • Location — only when you tap to use it, only for the forecast, and approximate is enough. Sprout never asks for background location.
  • Notifications — only if you turn reminders on.
  • Camera and photos — only when you add a photo to a plant. Photos stay on the device.

Children

Sprout is not directed at children and collects nothing that could identify anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything, because everything is on the device. In the browser, clearing site data does the same. You can clear a saved location on its own from Profile at any time. There is no server copy to request the deletion of.

Changes

If what Sprout does with your data ever changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves. It is written from the code rather than around it, so it cannot quietly drift out of date.
