# Sprout Evergreen — Character Pack

Sprout is the selected botanical companion for Sprout Evergreen.
The approved base is the content jade leaf spirit with a pale mint heart-shaped face, curling leaf crown and two broad leaf arms.

The pack is 17 images: the base, four care scenes, four app scenes, three weather scenes and five emotions. They sit in one flat folder, named `sprout-<name>.png`, so the paths the app, the site and `CLAUDE.md` already point at stay where they are.

## Visual direction

Jade green and pale mint on a deep forest green background; clean rounded botanical shapes and a gentle dimensional finish. The intended background colour in the generation brief was #0B2119; generated colours can vary slightly. Keep the base proportions and identity in every new image. Accessories may hide the crown, as the hardhat does in the planner scene.

## Usage notes

These are static PNG images with their original backgrounds, not transparent cutouts or animations. Original image bytes are preserved. Keep their square aspect ratio and avoid stretching. Make every new pose from sprout-base.png so the character keeps the same identity.

Nothing in the app reads this folder. The app ships its own 360 × 360 WebP plates in `img/sprout/`, resized from these, and only for the poses it places:
