# Sprout Evergreen — Character Pack

Sprout is the selected botanical companion for Sprout Evergreen.
The approved base is the content jade leaf spirit with a pale mint heart-shaped face, curling leaf crown and two broad leaf arms.

## Suggested uses

- **sprout-base.png**: Default character, welcome messages and general brand use.
- **sprout-new-growth.png**: Growth milestones, new plants and completed care tasks.
- **sprout-watering.png**: Watering reminders and care guidance.
- **sprout-concerned.png**: Plant health checks and gentle attention prompts.
- **sprout-resting.png**: Evening messages, quiet states and all-tasks-complete screens.

## Visual direction

Jade green and pale mint on a deep forest green background; clean rounded botanical shapes and a gentle dimensional finish. The intended background colour in the generation brief was #0B2119; generated colours can vary slightly.

## Usage notes

These are static PNG images with their original backgrounds, not transparent cutouts or animations. Original image bytes are preserved. Keep their square aspect ratio and avoid stretching. Use sprout-base.png as the reference for future poses and expressions to retain the same character identity.
