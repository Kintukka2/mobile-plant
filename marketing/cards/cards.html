Trim
90 × 55 mm, 2.2 mm corner radiusAustralian standard size. Supply with 3 mm bleed all round and keep type 4 mm inside the trim.
Stock
600 gsm triplex, jade centre plyThe coloured middle sheet shows as a fine green line on the card edge, so a stack of them in a pocket still reads as Sprout.
Ground
Viridium emerald, both sidesClosest spot match is Pantone 3435 C. Proof a rich CMYK build against it before the run; deep greens drift warm on press.
Finish
Soft-touch matt laminateVelvet to the hand, which is what makes someone keep holding it long enough to turn it over.
Deboss
Blind, both sidesNo ink. The front logo and the large leaf on the back are pressed into the card. One die per side, shared by all seven cards. 600 gsm triplex is thick enough that neither press shows through on the other face; confirm on the printer's sample.
Foil
Champagne gold, both sidesFront: the question mark at the end of the hook. Back: the “Scan to” line, set at 7pt so the foil holds on tracked caps.
QR
19 mm, version 4, error correction HDark on a pale mint plate with a 3-module quiet zone, square finders and rounded data dots; it decodes cleanly at every scale I tested. The 7 × 7 module centre carries the mark; level H recovers 30%, the knockout takes under 5%. Opens sproutevergreen.com.
Type
Hatton, Cormorant, JostThe hook is Hatton Ultralight at 6.3 mm, a size up from the medium so its hairlines survive the press; proof it on the actual stock before the run. The SPROUT wordmark on the back stays Hatton Medium. The call to action is Jost in champagne, the same ink as the question mark on the front.
Character
Looking over the QR plateThe site's own corner cut-out at 16 mm, mirrored, set behind the plate so the plate hides its flat cropped edge and Sprout is holding up the code.
