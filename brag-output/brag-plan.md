# Sprout: launch video plan

**What it is:** a houseplant app that works out each plant's care from the species, the pot, the
room's light and the season, instead of asking how often to remind you.
**For:** anyone with more than a couple of houseplants who has lost one to a fixed schedule.
**Sets it apart:** it explains its own numbers ("Why 10 and not 8?"), diagnoses by species,
and reads the light in your home from a plan you trace.
**Most impressive claim:** the site's own headline. *Most plant apps ask how often you want
reminding. This one works it out.*
**Visual hook:** that line, set big in PP Hatton 200 on Sprout's near-black green field.
**Real UI shown:** Today → Monny → the watering card's "why"; Diagnose → yellow leaves → ranked
causes; the planner shading the floor for a plant.
**Share caption:** see `share-copy.txt`.

## Angle and tone
One video for the whole app, cut as three feature cards. `app-store` in shape (clean cards,
smooth slides between them), `polished` in manner, because Sprout's brand is restrained: no
exclamation marks, emerald `#3FBE8B` used only for meaning, Hatton 200 for display.

**Format:** 1080×1920, 30fps, 23.2s. Vertical, so it can go to Reels/Shorts and stand in as a
store preview cut.

## Sound
The reels' piece: D major at 84 BPM, soft pad, a sparse plucked pulse, taps tuned to the chord, a
breath of filtered noise for each push and slide, all through one shared room. −16 LUFS.

## Honesty notes
Demo data only: the reels' seeded six-plant greenhouse under a fixed clock (Sat 3 Oct 2026,
08:12 in Sydney). Every number on screen is the app computing from that data. Store availability
is worded exactly as the marketing site words it.
